Sources and gaps

  • Old Cars Data, i8 price history: yearly medians of completed US auctions (Bring a Trailer, Cars & Bids, Mecum and others), plus per-model-year sale lists.
  • Classic.com, i8 market: dated auction results and dealer listings, including last asking prices of cars that sold or were pulled.
  • WardsAuto on the Manheim index and Cox Automotive: the industry-wide used-car boom and correction.
  • InsideEVs (record sale), Consumer Guide (facelift).

Where data is thin

  • Before 2021, only a handful of public auction sales exist each year, and none in 2018. Those yearly medians are shown hollow and dashed; treat them as rough.
  • Individual sale records only go back to late 2021, and most are from 2024 on. Private-party and dealer sale prices are not public.
  • Asking-price history before mid-2025 is not public without paid tools, and listings from mid-February to May 2026 could not be retrieved, so Chart 2 has a gap there.
  • Most records do not say coupe or roadster. 2014–2017 cars are all coupes; 2019–2020 cars count as coupe or roadster only when the listing says so, so the Roadster filter shows almost nothing.
  • Two early-2026 sales are listed as sold by one source and unsold at their high bid by another; their tooltips say so. Two sales far below every other (likely non-running or salvage) are shown but left out of every figure.

          Notes by brand

          • Porsche: the 997.2 (2009–12) avoids the 997.1's IMS bearing and bore-scoring worries. Budget $2k–$3.5k a year plus big items (clutch $3k–$5k, PDK rebuild $4k–$8k) (Tyre Press). 911 values are softening as 992 prices fall (RennPulse).
          • G-Wagon: only the old-body 2016–18 G550 fits; a 2019+ is about $83k (KBB). 13 mpg. Watch door seals, suspension bushings, transfer case noise (Autoscore).
          • Lotus: the Emira isn't a $60k car in the US yet (cheapest ask $88,995) and early builds had faults (PistonHeads). Elise values rose 55% over 3 years (Hagerty). Service near Greenville is unconfirmed — call before buying.
          • Corvette C8: early cars had valve-spring, transmission and no-start issues, mostly fixed under warranty; check the valve-spring bulletin on June–Sept 2020 builds (8020 Automotive).
          • Out of budget: Lexus LC 500 ($76k+ asks), Lotus Emira ($89k+), Mercedes-AMG GT (~$72k+), Acura NSX (~$130k), G-Wagon 2019+ (~$83k).

          From a deep research pass on Oct 9, 2026. Several prices are asks or KBB values, not sales. Get Greenville insurance quotes on your top 2–3; that will likely decide it.
